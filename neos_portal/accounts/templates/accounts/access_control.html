What each level can do

Level 1 — View: sees everything; can't add, edit, download or approve.

Level 2 — Add & edit: sees everything; can add, edit and download. What it adds (manifests, assets, personnel, SGS reports, maintenance reports) waits for a Level 3 to approve it, and removals are requests.

Level 3 — Approve: everything Level 2 can, and approves or rejects the work waiting in Approvals, removes records, and approves removal requests. Its own work is approved automatically.

Admin: Level 3, plus this page (accounts and levels).

Every-change alerts — switch on for a manager to be notified of everything other people add, upload, change or delete. Linking an account to its employee lets it receive “assigned to you” alerts.
